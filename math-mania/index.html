Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application MathMania developed and published by Random Basic Apps.

MathMania respects your privacy. While the app can be used offline for gameplay, it uses third-party advertising services to support development.

Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with ads, third-party advertising SDKs may collect the following types of data:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Approximate location (based on IP)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

MathMania itself does not directly collect, store, or sell personal data.

Third-Party Services

MathMania uses advertising mediation provided by LevelPlay to serve and optimize ads from multiple ad networks.

These third-party ad networks may collect data as described in their own privacy policies.

Data Usage

Collected data is used for:

  • Serving personalized or contextual ads
  • Measuring ad performance
  • Preventing fraud and abuse

Children's Privacy

MathMania does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads shown may be subject to platform-level age restrictions and policies.

Your Choices

You may opt out of personalized advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
